# Day 15 – Networking Concepts: DNS, IP, Subnets & Ports

## 🎯 Objective

Understand how devices communicate over a network using:

- **DNS** → Resolves domain names to IP addresses
- **IP Addressing** → Identifies devices on a network
- **CIDR & Subnets** → Defines and divides IP networks
- **Ports** → Identifies services running on a device
